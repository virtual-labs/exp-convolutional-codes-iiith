### Textbooks

**1. Error Control Coding**<br/>
BY: S. Lin and D. Costello - 2nd Edition, Prentice-Hall, 2004<br/>
_Comprehensive coverage of convolutional codes, trellis diagrams, Viterbi decoding algorithm, and sequential decoding techniques._

**2. Fundamentals of Convolutional Coding**<br/>
BY: R. Johannesson and K. Zigangirov - 2nd Edition, Wiley Press, 2015<br/>
_Detailed treatment of convolutional code structures, distance properties, and optimal decoding algorithms._

**3. Digital Communications**<br/>
BY: John G. Proakis and Masoud Salehi - 5th Edition, McGraw-Hill, 2008<br/>
_Excellent coverage of convolutional encoding, maximum likelihood decoding, and soft-decision Viterbi algorithm._

**4. Convolutional Codes and Their Performance in Communication Systems**<br/>
BY: A. J. Viterbi and J. K. Omura - IEEE Press, 1979<br/>
_Classic reference on convolutional codes with focus on implementation and performance analysis._

**5. The Theory of Error-Correcting Codes**<br/>
BY: F. J. MacWilliams and N. J. A. Sloane - North-Holland, 1977<br/>
_Fundamental algebraic treatment of convolutional codes and their properties._
